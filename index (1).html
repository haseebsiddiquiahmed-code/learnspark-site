🌱 Waqf-e-Jadid Learning Hub

A student-friendly place to learn about the purpose, history, and service of Waqf-e-Jadid.

What is Waqf-e-Jadid?

Waqf-e-Jadid is a community-wide scheme of financial sacrifice and service that supports the spread of Islamic teachings, especially in areas where educational and religious resources are limited. It encourages members—including young people—to contribute with sincerity, responsibility, and a spirit of service.

Learn about the scheme through trusted community sources and guidance from the institution's leadership.

📖 Learn the basics

  • Purpose: help support religious education, outreach, and community service.
  • Spirit: give willingly and thoughtfully, according to one's circumstances.
  • Participation: families and young people can learn, encourage good habits, and take part in age-appropriate ways.
  • Reflection: focus on sincerity, gratitude, and helping others—not competition.

🧭 Study pathway

  1. Read a short introduction.
  2. Explore its history and aims.
  3. Listen to relevant guidance and talks.
  4. Write down three important lessons.
  5. Review the quiz below.

🤝 How young people can help

  • Understand the purpose before explaining it to others.
  • Share accurate information respectfully.
  • Make a personal plan with a parent or guardian if you wish to participate financially.
  • Pray for the success of sincere service and educational efforts.
  • Show generosity through time, kindness, and helpful actions too.

📝 Notes to remember

After studying, write your own answers:

  • What is the main purpose of Waqf-e-Jadid?
  • Who can learn about and support the scheme?
  • What values does it encourage?
  • What is one thing I learned today?
